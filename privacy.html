The short version

We collect your email address when you join our waitlist. We use it to send you updates about VÉLO. We don't sell it, share it with third parties for advertising, or do anything creepy with it. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we are

VÉLO is a pre-launch skincare brand based in the United States. We're building an SPF50 active skin veil mist for women who move. You can reach us at hello@getvelo.com.

What we collect

When you join our waitlist, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • The date and time of your submission
  • Basic technical data from your browser (IP address, browser type) — collected automatically by our hosting provider, Netlify

We do not collect your name, payment information, or any other personal details unless you provide them voluntarily.

How we use it

Your email is used only to:

  • Notify you when VÉLO is available to purchase
  • Share early access opportunities, founder pricing, and product updates
  • Occasionally ask for your feedback to help shape the product

We will never send you unrelated marketing, sell your email to any third party, or use it for advertising purposes.

Analytics

This website uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics tool. Plausible does not track individuals, does not use cookies, and is fully GDPR compliant. No data is shared with Google, Meta, or any advertising platform.

Hosting & data storage

This website is hosted on Netlify. Waitlist form submissions are stored securely in Netlify's form service. You can review Netlify's privacy policy at netlify.com/privacy.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data at any time
  • Unsubscribe from all communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@getvelo.com and we'll respond within 5 business days.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies. Plausible Analytics is cookie-free. We do not serve any third-party cookies or tracking pixels.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and, if the change is material, notify you by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions? We're a small team and we read every email. Reach us at hello@getvelo.com.
