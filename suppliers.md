## Vetting Script: Questions to Ask Every Supplier

### SPF / Zinc Oxide
- What zinc oxide % options achieve SPF50 in third-party testing?
- Do you use **non-nano** zinc oxide? (Required for "reef-safe" / "clean" claims)
- Do you provide an SPF testing report (ISO 24444 or FDA method)?
- Is the formula eligible for US OTC sunscreen marketing under the FDA Monograph?

### Tint / Adaptive Coverage
- Do you offer **universal tint** or adaptive tint that adjusts to multiple skin tones?
- What is the white cast level at SPF50 zinc concentrations?
- Can tint be matched to a custom color standard?

### Custom Fragrance
- Can a custom or proprietary fragrance be incorporated into the SPF50 formula?
- Do certain fragrance compounds reduce SPF efficacy or destabilize the formula?
- Do you have an in-house fragrance library, or can we supply our own fragrance raw materials?
- Is fragrance addition subject to additional stability testing? Cost + timeline?

### Mist / Spray Format
- Is this available in a **non-aerosol pump mist** format? (Aerosol SPF sprays have a separate FDA review pathway — avoid for now)
- Has SPF efficacy been validated specifically in the mist delivery format?

### FDA Compliance
- Is your facility FDA-registered?
- Can you provide a full US OTC label formatted with a **Drug Facts panel**?
- Do you have existing US export experience for SPF products?

### Costs & Timeline
- What is the sample cost and lead time for a custom tinted SPF50 mist?
- What is the production lead time from formula approval to shipping?
- What is the per-unit cost at 200 / 500 / 1,000 units?
- What is included in the base price (formula, filling, packaging, testing)?
